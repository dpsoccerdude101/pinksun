02 — The building

What is inside

Six dedicated courts on our own surface, under our own ceiling. Nothing is shared with a basketball league or a birthday party.

Courts and surface
Six cushioned acrylic courts with permanent nets and painted lines. Court spacing is generous enough to chase a lob without ending up on the next court.

Lockers and showers
Day lockers are free, bring your own lock or rent one at the desk. Four showers, towels at the front desk.

Café
Coffee, water, and a bench long enough for four people to sit and argue about a call. Opens at 6:15 AM and closes with the building.

Pro shop
Balls, grips, and demo paddles you can play with for a session before buying. Members get ten percent off.

Mezzanine
Seating above courts 4 through 6 for anyone waiting, watching, or avoiding a third game in a row.

Accessibility
Step-free entrance, accessible restrooms, and two accessible parking spaces by the main door.

03 — Getting here

Directions

We are on the north side of the building, second driveway past the light.

From the village line
Take the county road north about a mile, then left onto Sunfield Way. Second driveway on the right.

From the village
Six minutes. Take Main Street west out of the village, then the same left onto Sunfield Way.

Parking
Sixty free spaces, plus overflow along the north edge of the lot. Do not park in the two spots marked for the neighboring tenant.
