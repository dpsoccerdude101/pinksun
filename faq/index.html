01 — First time

Playing for the first time

Nobody in this building started out good.

Do I need to be good at this?+

No. Open play runs in three bands, 3.0, 3.5 and 4.0 and up, and a host puts you on a court with people near your level. There are no tryouts and no cuts.

If you are brand new, come to the 9:00 AM block on a weekday. It is the quietest session and the host has time to walk you through the rules.

Do I need a partner?+

Not for open play. You show up, you get rotated into games, and you will play with a dozen different people in ninety minutes. Leagues are the only thing that needs a fixed partner, and we will pair you with someone if you sign up solo.

What are the actual rules?+

Serve underhand below the waist, diagonal to the opposite court, and let it bounce. The first two shots of every rally must bounce: the return of serve, and the third shot. The seven-foot zone on either side of the net is the kitchen, and you cannot volley in it.

Scoring is to eleven, win by two, and only the serving side scores. That is enough to start playing.

Do I need my own paddle?+

No. Loaner paddles are at the desk and they are decent. The pro shop keeps demo paddles you can play with for a session before buying anything.

02 — Money

Prices and membership

Every price is published on the join page. No call-for-pricing.

What does it cost to try it?+

Twelve dollars for a drop-in open-play block, and free for your first session if you live in Fairport. That includes a paddle and a ball.

When does a membership pay for itself?+

Monthly is $79, which is about seven drop-in blocks, or two hours of court rental at the members' rate. If you play twice a week, monthly is cheaper than paying per visit.

The $749 founding seat is for people who rent courts regularly: at $15 an hour versus $40, twenty-five hours of court time covers the whole difference for the year.

Can I cancel a membership?+

Monthly cancels any month with no fee and no phone call. The annual founding seat runs for the year at a locked rate.

03 — Logistics

Getting here and getting in

Hours, parking and directions are on the visit page.

Where do I park?+

Free lot at the door, sixty spaces, with entrances at both ends of the building. Overflow runs along the north edge of the lot. We are two minutes off Route 31.

Is there a dress code?+

Non-marking court shoes and a shirt. That is the whole list. Courts are cushioned acrylic, so running shoes are fine but they will feel slow.

How do I book, exactly?+

One account on our booking system, created once. After that you pick a block, confirm, and show up ten minutes early. Every session we run is published with its level and how many spots are left.
