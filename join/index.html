03 — First visit

The six questions
everyone asks

The full list of questions, including money and logistics, is on the FAQ page.

Do I need to be good at this?+

No. Open play runs in three bands, 3.0, 3.5 and 4.0 and up, and a host puts you on a court with people near your level. There are no tryouts and no cuts.

Do I need a partner?+

Not for open play. You show up, you get rotated into games, and you will play with a dozen different people in ninety minutes. Leagues are the only thing that needs a fixed partner.

What does it cost to try it?+

Twelve dollars for a drop-in block, and free for your first session if you live in Wren Hollow. That includes a paddle and a ball.

Do I need my own paddle?+

No. Loaner paddles are at the desk and they are decent. If you keep coming back, the pro shop has demo paddles you can play with for a session before buying anything.

Can I cancel a membership?+

Monthly cancels any month with no fee. The annual founding seat runs for the year, and the rate is locked for that year.

Where do I park?+

Free lot at the door, sixty spaces, with entrances at both ends of the building. We are two minutes off the village line.

Start with one block.

Twelve dollars, ninety minutes, paddle included, first session free for Wren Hollow residents. If it is not for you, you have lost nothing.
