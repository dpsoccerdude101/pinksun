01

Open play, levelled

Six courts split by rating band, rotating every eleven minutes. A host on court 6 keeps the queue moving, matches you with people near your level, and calls the scores. This is the cheapest way to find out if you like it here.

Bring nothing. Paddles and balls are at the desk.

Clinics and coaching

Ninety minutes, four players, one coach. Third-shot drops, dinking patterns, stacking and switching, and the reset you keep missing. Video review on request at no extra cost. Private coaching is the same coach with the court to yourself.

Bring your own paddle if you have one. Loaners are fine too.

04

Court rental

Reserve one court or all six by the hour. Balls, paddles and a scoring tablet come with it, and you control who is on the court. Members rent at $22 and founding members at $15, which is usually the point at which the membership pays for itself.

Bring four. If you are short a player, rent a court and join the open-play queue instead.

05

Junior program

Saturday mornings for ages 8 to 14, capped at twelve players so every kid gets strokes on the ball. Six-week block covering paddle control, court movement, and enough games that they ask to come back. Paddles sized for juniors are included.

Water bottle and non-marking shoes. That is it.

Booking

How it works

Three steps, once. After that a reservation takes about twenty seconds.

Create your account

One time, on our booking system. Name, email, phone and a waiver. No card needed until you actually reserve something.

Pick your block

Every session we run is published with its level, its court count and how many spots are left. You can see all of it before you commit.

Show up

Arrive ten minutes early. Paddles, balls and a court assignment are waiting at the desk, and the host will point you at the right court.
